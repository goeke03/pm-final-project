## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** A consumer in shift or variable work who cannot pay the balance in full and tries to set up an instalment plan in a short break.
- **Primary metric:** The agreement rate for variable-or-shift starters. The baseline is 41.6% on 2,862 starters. Fixed salary is 48.1%. It moves in the month of the journey, before the 60-day result, and it is the published gap for this consumer. Count it as the gap closing only while the guardrail holds. The overall agreement rate rose from 43.1% to 46.0% in the same months the hold got worse.
- **Moment of misery:** On a break, the journey asks for income and they do not know whether that means this month or a normal month, so they close it and mean to come back (N-01).
- **Guardrail:** The share of new agreements with no missed payment within 60 days. It must not drop. The baseline is 81.0% (3,115 of 3,846). From the miss rates, the earlier months were 84.8%, 84.1%, and 84.4% while 12 months was shown first, then 81.6%, 81.2%, and 81.0% after the 6 month plan was shown first. For variable or shift income, the current hold is 77.5%, from the 22.5% miss rate. This is the book of plans that already pay.
